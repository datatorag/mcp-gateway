Google's consent screen shows its permission checkboxes unticked. If you press Continue
without ticking them, Google connects nothing, and until now that left you back on the
dashboard under a small banner. Four changes around that moment.

- **If Google connects nothing, you land on a page that says so.** It names what happened,
  shows the box to tick (Select all), lists the services it covers, and has one button: **Try
  again**.
- **Try again goes straight back to the consent screen.** It used to send you through
  Google's account chooser first, which felt like signing in all over again. It now returns
  to the consent screen for the account you just picked. You can still switch accounts there.
- **A note under the Google connect button** says what the button opens, to tick every box,
  and that you can disconnect any time. Hide it once and it stays hidden on every device you
  sign in on. It comes back by itself if a connect returns with nothing granted.
- **The agent's first screen numbers its steps.** For an account with nothing connected it
  now reads "1. Connect an account" and "2. Once you connect, ask for something like", so the
  first thing to do is not left to guesswork.
