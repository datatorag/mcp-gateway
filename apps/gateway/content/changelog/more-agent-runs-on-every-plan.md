---
title: "Pro now includes 400 agent runs a month, and Free 40"
date: "2026-10-11"
tags: ["pricing", "billing"]
---

The monthly allowance of agent runs goes up on both plans. Pro goes from 100 to 400, and Free
goes from 25 to 40. Prices do not change.

The higher number applies to the period you are in now, on either plan. There is nothing to do.
The Billing page in the dashboard and the meter in the Agent show it against your usage.

Nothing else moves. The tool call allowances stay where they were, 10,000 a month on Pro and 250
on Free, and so does the ceiling on what a single run can use.
