---
title: "Errors on our side are no longer counted against your plan"
date: "2026-10-26"
tags: ["billing", "usage"]
---

A tool call that fails because of us, or because of the service behind it, no longer counts
toward your monthly calls. That covers:

- Google, Atlassian or Notion answering with a server error, or refusing for rate.
- A request that never reached the service, such as a dropped connection or a timeout.
- A failure inside DataToRAG itself.
- A Google refusal for access we never asked you for, when you granted everything we did ask for.

A call that fails because of the request still counts, as it did before: a wrong argument,
access you chose not to grant, something that does not exist, or any other refusal by the
service of what was asked.
