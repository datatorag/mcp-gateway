The dashboard now has a Playground. Example prompts don't just sit there
anymore: hit Run and a Claude-powered agent executes them against your
connected Google Workspace and Atlassian accounts, choosing tools and
streaming results live. It's a demo surface for trying the connectors
before you wire up your own client, with about 20 free runs per account.
Every response gets a thumbs up/down so you can tell us what worked and
what didn't.

The client setup wizard is rebuilt too, with step-by-step flows for
Claude web, Claude Desktop, Claude Code, Cursor, and ChatGPT. A live
status check flips to green when your first tool call comes through, so
you know the connection works before you close the tab.
