Pick the agent you use, and you get its one step to connect DataToRAG. The same block is now on
the Agent page, the dashboard home, the getting-started docs, the home page, and at the end of
every blog post, and it has its own page at [Add DataToRAG to your agent](/add-to-your-agent).

- **Every client, one step.** Claude, Claude Desktop, Claude Code, ChatGPT, Codex, Cursor, or any
  other MCP client. Each gets one line saying where it goes and one button to copy what to paste.
  Cursor also gets a link that opens Cursor with DataToRAG filled in.
- **On the Agent page,** where you land after signing in, it is step 2, between connecting an
  account and asking for something.
- **No account first.** From the site or the docs you can add DataToRAG to your agent before you
  have an account: your agent asks you to sign in when it connects.
- **It remembers your pick** in this browser, and once you are signed in it marks the agents that
  have connected.
- **Plan limits, said up front.** Where a client's own docs limit custom connectors by plan, the
  block says so: Claude's Free plan allows one custom connector, and in ChatGPT full access needs
  a Business, Enterprise, or Edu workspace.
- **ChatGPT's steps are current.** ChatGPT now adds a custom MCP server under Plugins, without
  developer mode.
