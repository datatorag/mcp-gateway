title: "Connecting Google: the real consent screen, and the two boxes to tick"
date: "2026-10-22"
tags: ["dashboard", "google-workspace"]
connector: "google-workspace"
---

When you connect a Google account from the dashboard, a window now opens over the page before
you go to Google. It shows screenshots of Google's own consent screens, with the two
boxes that matter marked:

- **Ongoing access.** On the first screen, Google offers to let the connection keep working
  without a review every six months. Tick it, or the connection stops at Google's review.
- **Select all.** On the next screen, every service starts unticked. Tick Select all, then
  continue.

Press **Continue to Google** when you are ready, or **Not now** to stay where you were. A connect
link opened from outside the dashboard, or in a new tab, shows the same screens on a page of
its own.
