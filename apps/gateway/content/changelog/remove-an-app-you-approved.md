---
title: "Remove an app you approved"
date: "2026-11-25"
tags: ["dashboard"]
---

When you add DataToRAG to Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, or another client, you approve it once and it
keeps its access. Until now the dashboard had no place to see those approvals or take one back.

- **The Connections page lists the apps you approved.** Under your connected accounts, each app
  shows the name it gave, where its sign-in returns to, the day you approved it, and the day its
  access was last renewed. The name is the app's own and we do not verify it, so an app that
  registered twice is listed twice, each with its own details.
- **Remove ends that app's access.** We revoke its tokens and delete the approval, so it can no
  longer use DataToRAG as you until you add it again and approve it. Your connected accounts and
  your other apps keep working.

[Security and privacy](/docs/security) says exactly what Remove does.
