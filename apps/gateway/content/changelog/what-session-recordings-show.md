---
title: "What a session recording of the dashboard shows"
date: "2026-10-11"
tags: ["site", "privacy"]
---

We use PostHog to record dashboard sessions so we can see where the product gets in the way. A
recording can show the conversation content we already store for you: your messages, the agent's
replies and the results of its tool calls. It never shows what you type into a field, and it never
shows your invoice download links. The [privacy policy](/privacy) now says so.
