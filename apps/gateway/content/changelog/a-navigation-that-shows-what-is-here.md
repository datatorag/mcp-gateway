The site's menu is now three groups. Product covers what DataToRAG does, what it connects to (Google
Workspace, Atlassian, and Notion), and why you can trust it. Solutions has a setup link for each
agent (Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, Cursor, and any other MCP client) and a page for each kind of work.
Resources holds the blog, the changelog, the docs, the FAQ, and contact.

It works from the keyboard, and on a phone it opens as a full-height sheet.
