---
title: "The note on the Google card now sits inside the card"
date: "2026-10-21"
tags: ["dashboard", "google-workspace", "bugfix"]
connector: "google-workspace"
---

The note on the Google Workspace card on your dashboard, the one that says what the connect
button opens and to tick every box, was drawn below the card's button row rather than in it.
Once you had an account connected it sat under Add account as a separate strip, cut off from
the card it belongs to.

It now sits inside the card, under the buttons, in the same band as Add account and Browse
tools (or under Connect, before you have connected anything). The wording is the same, and
hiding it works the same way: hide it once and it stays hidden on every device you sign in on.
