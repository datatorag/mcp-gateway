---
title: "Connecting an account takes you straight to what it can do"
date: "2026-10-26"
tags: ["agent", "connections"]
---

Two changes to the moment you connect an account.

**You land in the agent, not on a settings page.** When you connect Google Workspace, Atlassian
or Notion from the dashboard, you now arrive in a new conversation with the agent that names the
account you just connected and offers two things it can do with it right away. Nothing runs until
you pick one or type something. If you started the connect from inside a conversation, you still
go back to that conversation, as before.

**A warning when the account is already connected to another DataToRAG login.** Your plan and
your usage follow the DataToRAG account you sign in with. If you sign in with one email, then
connect a work account that is already connected under a different DataToRAG login, the two
logins quietly split your usage. Now the connect stops and tells you, showing the other login's
address partly hidden, so you can sign in with that one instead or connect the account here
anyway.
