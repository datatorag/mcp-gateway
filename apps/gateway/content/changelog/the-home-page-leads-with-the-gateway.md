The top of the [home page](/) is now a picture of what the product is: DataToRAG in the middle,
and the tools it connects turning around it. Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Slides,
Contacts, Tasks, Jira, Confluence, and Notion each come to the front in turn, with one line on
what your agent can do there.

- Click a tool to bring it to the front, or use the arrow keys. Its name links to its docs page.
- The wheel holds still if your system asks for reduced motion, and while your mouse rests on it.
- The comparison with Claude's built-in connectors now comes right after it, and the full table
  is where it was, further down the page.
