---
title: "A feed for the blog, and every post as Markdown"
date: "2026-11-25"
tags: ["site"]
---

The blog has a feed at [/blog/feed.xml](/blog/feed.xml): every post, newest first, with its
title, link, date, and description. Point a feed reader at it to see a post the day it is
published.

Every post is also served as Markdown at its own address plus `.md`, for example
`/blog/composio-alternative.md`: the post's title, its description, the days it was published
and last edited, the body, and its edit history at the end. [/llms.txt](/llms.txt) lists every
post with its Markdown address, beside the docs pages and use cases it already listed.

On a post that has been edited, the date line now says when, and the note at the end shows the
latest edit with the earlier ones folded under "Earlier edits".
