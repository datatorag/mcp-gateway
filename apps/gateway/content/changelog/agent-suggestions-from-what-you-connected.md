---
title: "Agent suggestions built from what you connected"
date: "2026-10-26"
tags: ["agent", "dashboard"]
---

The suggestions on the agent's first screen, and the cards on your dashboard, now come from what
your connected accounts can actually do, instead of one fixed list for everyone.

- **Only what you can run.** A suggestion appears only when the services it needs are connected
  and granted. Connect only Calendar and you will not be offered an email task.
- **Things that work on a new account first.** Turning pasted notes into a formatted Google Doc,
  pasted data into a Google Sheet, or a look at this week's calendar, before anything that needs
  months of history.
- **The inbox summary when there is an inbox to summarize.** It is offered once your inbox holds
  mail from people, not only sign-in alerts and promotions.
- **Doing ahead of reading.** Suggestions that create something, or move work from one service to
  another, come first. With two Google accounts connected, a suggestion can cover both inboxes.
- **At most one of your file names, in the last slot.** When Drive is connected, the last
  suggestion may name one of your recent files.

When you ask the agent something that is not about your connected accounts, it now says in a
line or two what it can do for you instead of answering a general question at length.
