---
title: "A Gmail message that cannot be found says why"
date: "2026-11-17"
tags: ["gmail", "gws-mcp"]
---

When your agent asks for a Gmail message, conversation, draft or attachment that Gmail cannot
find, the answer now says which one and in which of your accounts, why that usually happens (for
example, it was deleted, or it is in another connected account), and how to find it again, instead of
Google's bare "not found".

More on how these answers work is [on the blog](/blog/not-found-and-what-to-do-next).
