---
title: "Approve each new app before it connects"
date: "2026-10-26"
tags: ["auth", "oauth"]
---

The first time a new app connects to DataToRAG, you now see a page that names the app and the
site it sends you back to, with Approve and Deny. If you approve, DataToRAG remembers it for that
app and you won't be asked again.
