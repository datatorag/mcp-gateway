title: "A Google file, event, contact or task that cannot be found says why"
date: "2026-11-17"
tags: ["gws-mcp", "drive", "docs", "sheets", "slides", "calendar", "contacts", "tasks"]
---

What Gmail started doing for messages, the rest of Google Workspace now does too. When your agent
asks for a Drive file, a Google Doc, Sheet or Slides deck, a calendar event, a contact or a task
list that Google cannot find, the answer says which one and in which of your accounts, why that
usually happens (for example, it was deleted, it is not shared with that account, or it is in
another connected account), and which tool finds it again, instead of Google's bare "not found".

When the id belongs to a different kind of Google file, such as a Doc id given to a Sheets tool,
the answer says what it is and which tools open it.
