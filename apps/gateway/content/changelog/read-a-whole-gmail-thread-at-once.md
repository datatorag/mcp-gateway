---
title: "Read a whole Gmail thread at once"
date: "2026-11-16"
tags: ["gmail", "gws-mcp"]
---

Claude and other agents can now read an entire email conversation in one go. Ask yours to read
the thread with a client about a renewal and it gets every message, oldest to newest, with each
message's text and attachments, drafts and anything in Trash included and marked as such. For a
long conversation it can look at just the senders, dates and a preview of each message, then read
the ones it needs.

The [Gmail docs](/docs/gmail) cover the new `gmail_read_thread` tool.
