New accounts that land on the agent page after signing up see one question: how did you find
DataToRAG? It is a free-text box with a Continue button and a Skip link, shown once and never
again. Answer or skip, one click either way, and the page is yours.

We ask because the answer tells us where to spend our time. Skipping it costs nothing.
