---
title: "One question after you sign up"
date: "2026-11-09"
tags: ["dashboard", "gateway"]
---

New accounts now see one question on their first landing: how did you find DataToRAG? It is a
free-text box with a Continue button and a Skip link, shown once and never again, and nothing
waits on the answer. The page behind it works as usual.

We ask because the answer tells us where to spend our time. Skipping it costs nothing.
